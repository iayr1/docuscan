SHORT:
Scan to PDF, extract text with OCR and edit PDFs – fully offline and private.
FULL:
DocuScan turns your phone into a fast, private document scanner and PDF toolkit. Everything runs on your device – no account, no ads, no cloud uploads.

SCAN ANYTHING
• Multi-page scanning with automatic edge detection and perspective correction
• Scan modes for documents, receipts, books, whiteboards, notes, certificates and photos
• ID card mode puts the front and back on one page
• Crop, rotate, and enhance with filters like black & white, grayscale and colour boost
• Read QR codes and barcodes

EXTRACT TEXT (OCR)
• On-device text recognition for English and Latin scripts, Hindi/Marathi (Devanagari), Chinese, Japanese and Korean
• Copy, share or save recognised text
• Create searchable PDFs and find any document by the words inside it

COMPLETE PDF TOOLKIT
• Images to PDF, merge, split and compress PDFs
• Add or remove PDF passwords
• Convert Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files to PDF
• Export PDFs to Word, Excel or plain text
• Add watermarks and print directly from the app

SIGN & ANNOTATE
• Draw and save your signature, add stamps, highlight, and redact sensitive details

ORGANISE
• Folders, favourites, tags, search and sorting
• Recycle bin with 30-day restore, duplicate finder and storage analyser

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Optional app lock with PIN, fingerprint or face unlock
• Lock individual documents
• Secure Vault keeps sensitive files encrypted with AES-256 on your device
• Works completely offline – your documents never leave your phone

A clean, modern design with light and dark themes makes managing paperwork simple.
